Apply your terrain profile to 2D noise.

Especially useful for DistanceS and DistanceExponential curves. Monotonic Manual curves are also supported.

  1. 1 Paste your height curveThe curve that currently maps height to density.
  2. 2 Copy the inverse curveUse it to map your 2D noise.
  3. 3 Add the linear height termThe terrain surface stays where the sum is zero.
How the calculation works
Find the surface height
H = C⁻¹(−noise2D)
Convert it to the noise output
G = −L(H)
Keep the same surface
G(noise2D) + L(Y) = 0

C is your original height curve. H is the surface height. L is the new linear height mapping. G is the exported noise curve. The required height mapping is shown below after you paste your nodes.
